About Worli Promenade

The Worli Promenade is a scenic 2.5 km waterfront walkway along the Arabian Sea in Mumbai. Famous for its stunning views of the Bandra–Worli Sea Link, it is a popular destination for cycling, jogging, walking, and enjoying beautiful sunsets. Rent a bicycle with us and experience one of Mumbai's most picturesque coastal rides.
